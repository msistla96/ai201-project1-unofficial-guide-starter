While in H1B status, can I invest in an existing company?
Answer
An H1B worker can make a passive investment in any company. However, it must genuinely be a passive investment only. One cannot work at the company or run or manage the business. Many people think they are not “working” because they hire someone else for the day-to-day work or management. However, hiring and supervising someone (even people who don’t need much supervision) generally is considered work. (06.May.2026)
