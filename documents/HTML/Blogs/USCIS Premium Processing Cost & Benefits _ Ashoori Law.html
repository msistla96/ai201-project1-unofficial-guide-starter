USCIS Premium Processing: Is it Worth it?

Author: Gabriella Manolache, Esq.

OVERVIEW

     

    Hello, readers! Today, we embark on a journey to unravel a less-discussed aspect of the premium processing service offered by the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS). In this article, we delve into the intricacies of premium processing, shedding light on considerations that are often overshadowed. If you are contemplating the use of premium processing for your case, stay tuned, as this perspective might offer you valuable insights.

    Decoding Premium Processing

    In a nutshell, USCIS premium processing is an optional service provided by USCIS for certain types of cases. It allows applicants to expedite the processing of their applications by paying an additional fee. The USCIS premium processing service is particularly useful for those who seek quicker responses from USCIS. For instance, if the standard processing time for an L1 Visa petition is six months, utilizing premium processing involves paying an extra fee, currently set at $2,500, to have the case reviewed within just 15 days.

    A New Angle on Premium Processing

    While the expedited processing offered by USCIS premium processing can be a game-changer, we are here to present an alternative viewpoint that is often overlooked when considering expedited USCIS processing. Many applicants are drawn to USCIS premium processing due to its accelerated timeline, but there’s more to consider before making a decision.

    The Time Factor and Its Implications

    When an applicant opts for the USCIS premium processing service, USCIS is legally obligated to issue a response within the designated time frame, usually 15 days for most eligible applications under USCIS Premium Processing. However, this doesn’t necessarily mean that a final decision will be made within this window. USCIS may either approve the case or issue a Request for Evidence (RFE), seeking additional information or clarification.

    The Potential for Request for Evidence

    Consider this scenario: you submit an extensive application under USCIS premium processing, potentially spanning hundreds of pages, for premium processing. The immigration officer assigned to your case also has a 15-day deadline to review and respond. Given the substantial amount of material to evaluate within a limited time, there is a possibility, albeit unofficial, that the chances of receiving an RFE could increase.

    Understanding the Unspoken Dynamics

    While there are no official statistics indicating a direct link between the premium processing service and a higher likelihood of receiving an RFE, discussions within the immigration community have pointed to a potential correlation. As a seasoned immigration lawyer, I’ve observed instances where cases under USCIS premium processing seemingly encountered a more pronounced likelihood of RFE issuance due to the time constraints placed on immigration officers.

    The Refund Quandary

    USCIS’s premium processing commitment isn’t just about expediting processing; it’s also about providing a response within the stipulated time. If the response isn’t issued within the designated window, USCIS is obligated to refund the premium processing fee. Naturally, USCIS aims to avoid this refund scenario.

    Considering Options

    In light of these dynamics, it’s essential to emphasize thapremium processing might still be a viable choice for certain cases. However, a measured and informed approach is crucial. Engaging with an experienced immigration lawyer becomes paramount to comprehending the intricate variables at play and ensuring that your decision aligns with your unique circumstances.

    Closing Thoughts and the Road Ahead

    In conclusion, our exploration of premium processing has revealed an angle that often remains in the shadows. Your decision to pursue USCIS premium processing shouldn’t solely rest on the allure of an expedited timeline. Rather, it should be grounded in a holistic understanding of the potential implications, with guidance from immigration professionals.

    Acknowledgment and Forward Momentum

    We extend our gratitude to you, our dedicated readers, for joining us on this journey of uncovering lesser-discussed aspects of premium processing. In the realm of immigration, knowledge is power, and we remain committed to providing you with insights that empower your decisions. As we march ahead, equipped with newfound perspectives, we look forward to guiding you through the multifaceted landscape of immigration regulations. Thank you for being a part of our community, and until our next endeavor!
